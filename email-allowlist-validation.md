# Google Email Authorization List Validation

The administrator account was verified as an executive in the preview access response. The **Team access** workspace is now available in the navigation and presents an **Authorized email list**.

The workspace allows an executive administrator to add an approved Google email address, assign the person to one of the five portfolio teams, set their team role, change an existing assignment, and revoke platform access. A revoked address loses both its team membership and access-list entry.

At sign-in, the Google callback accepts only the configured executive administrator email or an email with an active authorization-list entry. Portfolio snapshot reads apply the same check so unapproved users cannot retrieve portfolio data through the platform API.
