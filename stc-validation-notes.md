# STC Visual Refresh Validation

The executive dashboard, portfolio workspace, and team-access workspace were reviewed at desktop and tablet viewport sizes. The refreshed interface uses a black and plum base, white editorial headings, purple navigation and financial chart signals, and restrained red emphasis for risk and investment attention. The team access form, directory, navigation, controls, and status accents remain readable against the revised surfaces.

The roadmap and financial preview captures initially displayed their loading states while the remote portfolio snapshot was warming; subsequent application requests complete normally. The team bootstrap process was cached and parallelized to reduce repeated loading time. The responsive information layout remains usable at the tablet breakpoint, with data-heavy roadmaps preserving horizontal scroll rather than compressing critical annual columns.

The live preview confirms the white `stc.` brand mark and full Architecture Master Roadmap name are visible in the top-left navigation. Relationship-map headings, selected-node labels, and editor copy now use Strategic Objectives terminology. Native selection lists explicitly use dark STC surfaces with white option text to prevent the low-contrast white popup observed previously.
